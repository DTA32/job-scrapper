## Job Description
- Promote and drive the development and implementation of new products, including value-added services and return-related products.
- Develop product promotion strategies to increase product adoption and contribute to company revenue growth.
- Analyze market and operational data to identify opportunities for product improvement and business growth.
- Collaborate with relevant teams to ensure smooth product implementation and promotion.

## Job Requirements
- Minimum HSK 5 or equivalent Mandarin proficiency.
- Good data analysis skills with basic knowledge of spreadsheet formulas.
- Experience in the logistics industry is preferred.
- Previous experience in product management is highly preferred.
- Strong communication, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
- Able to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams.
