## Job Description
- Responsible for evaluating the efficiency and performance of direct-operated personnel.
- Responsible for conducting cost data analysis for direct-operated business units.
- Assist in developing and optimizing operational data dashboards and reporting templates.
- Provide data-driven insights to support decision-making and improve operational efficiency.

## Job Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in any major
- Minimum HSK Level 5 proficiency.
- At least 1 year of relevant working experience.
- Proficient in using Excel (advanced functions preferred).
- Strong data analysis and data structuring capabilities.
