We are looking for a skilled and passionate Senior Backend Engineer who will be responsible for our server-side application logic along with its integration with the front-end platforms. In this position, you will use your programming skills and tools to analyze and translate business/feature requirements into well-structured, efficient, scalable software. You will also be actively involved in researching new technologies/tools that can be incorporated to improve the current system.

## What You Will Do
- Build solid, secure, performant and scalable APIs to serve our front-end platforms
- Optimize existing codes to improve performance and efficiency
- Collaborate with Product, QA and UI team to deliver features and improvements
- Implement best practices in code quality, security and data protection.

## Requirements
- Proficiency in at least one of the following programming languages: Go, C++, Java, .NET, or Python, demonstrating a deep understanding of their paradigms, constructs, and idioms with a minimum of 3 years of hands-on experience
- Experience in developing scalable Microservices and their associated components, such as tracing and logging, showcasing an adept understanding of strategies to handle high traffic loads
- Proven track record of implementing best practices including Clean Architecture, adherence to SOLID principles, and a strong foundation in Unit Testing
- Hands-on experience in managing Event Stream Processing, particularly with Kafka
- Familiarity and proficiency in modern version control systems such as GitLab or GitHub
- Experience working with AWS/GCP Managed services like SQS, DynamoDB, SES, etc., showcasing proficiency in leveraging cloud services for robust backend development
- Comfortable operating within a Linux environment, demonstrating fluency in its operations
- Self-motivated, passionate, and committed to stay updated with the latest trends and advancements in backend development
- Exceptional communication and teamwork skills and effective collaboration with team members
- Strong self-management abilities, especially in meeting project timelines and milestones
