We are looking for a Product Manager who will be responsible for managing the product development process end-to-end from identifying user and business needs, conducting product research, defining product requirements, prioritizing initiatives, coordinating with cross-functional teams, to monitoring product performance and evaluating outcomes after implementation.

Requirements

- Proven experience in Product Management, preferably handling B2C, B2B, marketplace, F&B, e-commerce, payment, loyalty, or digital platform products at least 1-3 years experience.
- Experience conducting user research and usability testing.
- Good understanding of Agile/Scrum and product development methodologies.
- Experience using Google Analytics or other product analytics tools.
- Strong ability to create product presentations/decks and present product strategy to stakeholders.
- Basic understanding of UI/UX Design and the ability to collaborate effectively with Product Designers.
- Familiarity with Figma or basic design skills is a plus.
- Strong analytical, communication, problem-solving, and stakeholder management skills.
- Able to manage multiple priorities and work effectively in a fast-paced environment

Job Descriptions :

- Identify and analyze user and business needs to define product opportunities and priorities.
- Conduct product research, user research, and usability testing to gather insights and validate product solutions.
- Develop clear and comprehensive Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) and product specifications.
- Define and prioritize product features, enhancements, and initiatives based on business impact, user needs, and available resources.
- Coordinate with Design, Engineering, Business, Operations, and other relevant stakeholders throughout the product development lifecycle.
- Translate business and user requirements into clear product solutions and actionable requirements.
- Collaborate with Product Designers to ensure user-friendly and effective product experiences.
- Monitor product performance using relevant metrics and product analytics tools.
- Evaluate product performance after implementation and identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
- Prepare product presentations and decks, and communicate product strategy, roadmap, and recommendations to stakeholders.
- Manage multiple product initiatives based on priorities, timelines, and business objectives.
