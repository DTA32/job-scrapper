## Job Overview
We are looking for a Data Analyst who can turn numbers and raw data into meaningful information and actionable insights. The role involves discovering patterns, identifying business opportunities, and helping stakeholders make better data-driven decisions.

## Qualifications
- Bachelor’s degree (S1) in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Information Systems, Business, or a related field.
- Minimum 1 year of experience as a Data Analyst or in a related analytical role.
- Strong proficiency in Microsoft Excel and SQL.
- Experience with Business Intelligence (BI) tools; Metabase is highly preferred.
- Curious and genuinely interested in working with numbers and data
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate data into meaningful business insights.
- Detail-oriented, with strong critical thinking and logical reasoning skills.
- Able to work independently as well as collaboratively within a team.
- Strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to clearly communicate analytical findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
