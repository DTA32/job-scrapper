As a Product Manager, you will be responsible for managing our medical product portfolio, developing marketing strategies, and supporting sales and business growth. You will also work closely with internal teams and external principals to ensure successful product execution in the market.

## Key Responsibilities
- Manage assigned medical products, including planning and monitoring performance
- Prepare and manage marketing materials
- Support and coordinate events, product trainings, and medical exhibitions
- Handle communication and coordination with principals
- Conduct market and competitor research to identify business opportunities
- Develop product positioning, pricing, and promotional strategies
- Coordinate with sales teams to provide product training and marketing support
- Monitor sales performance and provide regular reports and insights

## Requirements
- Bachelor’s degree in Biology, Biotechnology, or other science related fields
- Minimum 2 year of experience in product management or marketing
- Experience in the medical industry is preferred
- Good command of English, both written and spoken
- Strong communication, analytical, and organizational skills
