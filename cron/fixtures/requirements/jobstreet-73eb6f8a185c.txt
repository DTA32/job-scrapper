## Job Descriptions
- Develop, test, deploy, and maintain business applications.
- Develop and support RPA and automation solutions.
- Manage database, API, and system integration.
- Support SAP-related application and integration activities.
- Troubleshoot system issues and provide user support.
- Apply secure coding, documentation, and ICT governance standards.

## Job Requirements
- D3/S1 in Information Technology, Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
- Minimum 2–3 years of experience in programming/application development.
- Proficient in C#, ASP.NET/.NET Core, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, or similar technologies.
- Good knowledge of SQL Server/Oracle and database management.
- Exposure to RPA, automation, or AI-assisted development is a plus.
- Basic knowledge of SAP and system integration.
- Good analytical, communication, and teamwork skills.
- Able to communicate in English.
