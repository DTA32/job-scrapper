## Key Responsibilities
- Support Senior Mining Engineers in the delivery of mining studies, technical assessments, project work, and proposal preparation.
- Provide technical and engineering support to local and global mining projects, including data collection, data validation, drafting, and design activities.
- Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, including engineers, designers, and other stakeholders, during study phases and project execution.
- Perform engineering calculations, data analysis, and technical evaluations under the guidance of senior engineers.
- Prepare drawings, technical reports, presentations, and project documentation in accordance with project requirements and company standards.
- Ensure assigned work is completed in compliance with project specifications, quality requirements, and safety standards
- Participate in site visits, field inspections, and operational reviews to support project activities and gain practical mining experience.
- Prepare and submit progress reports, technical documentation, and other project deliverables as required.

## Qualifications
- Bachelor’s degree in mining or Geology
- Minimum of 0-4 years of experience in the mining industry, preferably with exposure to geo-mine engineering or mine operations and preferably with 1 or 2 years consultancy type of work.
- Preferably to have experience in underground mining or Surface mining either coal or minerals.
- Demonstrated mining engineering expertise, with professional certifications such as JORC, SNI, and POP considered an advantage.
- Proficiency in mining software packages such as Deswik, Vulcan, AutoCAD, and Rocscience. Experience in geological, geotechnical, or mine design modeling is considered an advantage.
- Fluent in writing and speaking English.
- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
- Excellent communication and teamwork abilities.
- Willingness to work on site.
- Indonesian nationality.
